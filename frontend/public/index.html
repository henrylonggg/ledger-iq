What you get

Every layer of a Quality of Earnings analysis.

Adjusted EBITDA bridge
Every one-time, non-recurring, and owner-related item identified and broken out in a clean bridge from reported to adjusted earnings.
Compliance checks
Automated review against ASC 606, ASC 842, and off-balance-sheet disclosure requirements. Issues surfaced with specific citations.
Trend analysis
Quarterly EBITDA and gross margin trends plotted over eight periods — reported vs. adjusted, with anomaly markers at inflection points.
Working capital analysis
DSO, inventory turns, and accrual timing reviewed against trailing seasonality. Peg recommendations included.
Risk scoring
Every finding rated 0–100 and graded red, amber, or green. Overall QoE score updates live as you accept or reject each flag.
AI narrative report
GPT-4o mini synthesizes all findings into a plain-language executive summary — customized to your approve/reject decisions.

ABOUT LEDGERIQ

LedgerIQ is an AI-powered Quality of Earnings platform built to streamline M&A due diligence. Upload financial documents and receive a full QoE report — scored, flagged, and graded — in seconds. Built on GPT-4o mini with a secure server-side architecture.

LedgerIQ is a demonstration platform. Output is informational only and does not constitute professional financial advice.
